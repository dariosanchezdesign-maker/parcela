How else Parcela makes money

Transaction

Professional referrals & project services

When a project hires a professional through Parcela's network, a referral fee supports keeping Property Intelligence free. Professionals never pay to rank higher — the Copilot recommends based on project fit, not payment.

Financial

Financing & lending referrals

The Financing Package is designed to be lender-ready. Parcela may eventually earn a referral fee when a project connects with a financing partner through that package — subject to applicable lending and referral regulations. Parcela does not itself originate loans or give financial advice.
