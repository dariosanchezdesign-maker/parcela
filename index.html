How it works

Three steps to a real answer

01

Tell it where and why

Enter an address and pick a goal — a home, an ADU, a business, or "I don't know."

02

It reads the property

Zoning district, lot geometry, flood mapping, coastal proximity, existing structures — cross-referenced together, not looked up one at a time.

03

You get ranked options

3–5 scenarios ranked by feasibility and complexity, plus what to investigate first.

Our method

We label what we don't know — on purpose

Property and zoning data is often incomplete or out of date. Parcela never quietly treats a guess as a fact — every finding is tagged by how sure we actually are.

Verified

Matched directly against a cadastral, zoning, or flood-mapping record.

Estimated

A reasonable read from available data — like structure condition from a recent assessment.

Assumption

Inferred logically, but not directly confirmed — flagged for a professional to verify.

Unknown

Not in this dataset at all. We say so, instead of guessing.
